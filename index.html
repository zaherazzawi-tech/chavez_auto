Our story

Family-owned for 33 years

Chavez Automotive Repair is a local, family-owned shop that has been taking care of cars and the people who drive them for 33 years. When you come in, you’re dealing with the family, not a call center.

From a check-engine light to a crumpled fender, we handle mechanical repair, maintenance and auto body under one roof, and we’ll always explain what your car needs before we start.
